# Constitution

The few principles this product must never break. Every stage checks its artefact against this file and writes down any judgement it made interpreting it. Keep it under one page. Edit it to fit your product.

1. **Truthful.** The product never shows a fact it did not capture, and never overclaims.
2. **Secure by default.** Least privilege for people and agents. No secrets in code, context or logs.
3. **Private.** Personal data is collected only for a stated purpose, and never used for tests.
4. **Accessible.** WCAG 2.2 AA on every screen.
5. **Reversible.** Every change ships behind a flag or with a tested rollback.
6. **Observable.** Every user journey has an SLO and an alert with a runbook.
7. **Humans decide.** Humans own every gate, every risk acceptance and every release.
